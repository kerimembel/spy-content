SPY

Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026 · Türkçe

SPY is a party word game made by Rimabel. The game runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no server that stores your games.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Player names, saved regulars, match history, statistics and your settings are written to this app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also wipe it from Settings → Reset inside the game.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

SPY contacts services for word pack updates, ads and purchases.

Word pack updates

The app checks a public catalog hosted by GitHub when it opens and returns to the foreground. The request does not include player names, match history or statistics. GitHub may receive technical connection data such as your IP address and request time. See the GitHub privacy statement. The last valid catalog is saved on your device so the game works offline.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve an ad, Google receives technical data such as your IP address, device and operating system information, and the ad identifiers your device exposes. SPY requests non-personalised ads, which means ad selection is not based on a profile built from your behaviour. SPY does not track you across other apps or websites and does not ask for the App Tracking Transparency permission.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, a consent form appears before the first ad. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options. Google's own handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.

Purchases

SPY+ purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. Purchase receipts are verified through RevenueCat, which assigns your installation a random anonymous identifier so your subscription can be restored on the same store account. No name, email or phone number is attached to it. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

PHOTOS

When you save a share card at the end of a night, the game asks for permission to add that image to your photo library. It is write-only. SPY never reads your existing photos.

MICROPHONE, CAMERA, LOCATION, CONTACTS

None of them are used or requested. The hints in SPY are spoken out loud at the table, not into the phone.

CHILDREN

SPY is rated 17+ because one of its packs contains adult content, and it is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

YOUR RIGHTS

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing on our side to export or delete. For ad-related data, the consent form and Google's controls apply. For purchase records, Apple, Google and RevenueCat act as processors of the store transaction. If you want any of this explained or acted on, write to us.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version appears on this page.
