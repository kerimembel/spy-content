One phone, three or more players, and one person holding a different word. Everything below is the short version of how it works and what to do when something goes wrong.

HOW A ROUND GOES

Add the players. Pass the phone around so everyone reads their word alone. Then, out loud and one at a time, each player gives a one-word hint about the word they were given. The spy has to fake it. When the table has had enough, vote. Get it right and the spy is out.

SPY+

What it unlocks

Every pack, including the after dark one, and no ads. It is available monthly, yearly, or as a one-time purchase that never expires.

I paid but the packs are still locked

Open Packs and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same App Store or Play account that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us with the store receipt and we will sort it out.

Cancelling a subscription

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SPY. On Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling stops the renewal; the packs stay open until the paid period ends. A one-time SPY+ purchase is not a subscription and cannot be cancelled.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. On iOS use reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android use the Play Store order history. We are happy to back up a refund request over email.

ADS

The free version shows an ad between games and an optional rewarded ad that opens one locked category for a single game. SPY+ removes all of them. If you are in the EEA or the UK you can change your ad consent choice from Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options.

THE GAME ITSELF

Changing the language

Settings → Language. English and Turkish word banks are written separately, so switching the language switches the words too, not just the interface.

Hiding the adult pack

Settings → Content. Set it back to general and the after dark pack disappears from the category list.

Deleting everything

Settings → Reset wipes players, history and statistics. Deleting the app does the same thing. Nothing of it lives on a server.

The app crashed or a word looks wrong

Email us with your device model, iOS or Android version, and what you were doing. Screenshots help. Word pair corrections are welcome, both languages.

PRIVACY

Read the full privacy policy. Short version: no account or microphone; player names and game history stay on your phone.
