SPY

Everyone gets the same word.
Except one.

One phone. Three or more players. No accounts, no second device.

Pass the phone around. Everyone reads their word alone. Then, out loud and one at a time, each player gives a one-word hint about the word they were given. One person is holding something different and does not know it either. Give hints, argue, vote, and find out who was faking.

What is inside

216 word pairs per language across 18 categories. Turkish and English banks written separately, not translated. Difficulty levels, a round timer, chaos mode with two spies, saved regulars, and a damage report at the end of the night.
